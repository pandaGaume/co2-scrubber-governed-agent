# WHAT MUST BE IMPOSSIBLE TO MISS

The viewer must understand these points even if they watch the video once, distracted, without reading the repository.

**1 — THIS IS A REAL RUN**

The agents' decisions are not pre-written for the video.

**LIVE SYSTEM — NOT A SCRIPTED DEMO**

must appear clearly on screen.

---

**2 — NVIDIA + NEBIUS**

Do not leave this to a tiny UI badge.

The narrator explicitly says:

> **NVIDIA Nemotron, running on Nebius Token Factory, supervises this station.**

And both names appear clearly on screen.

---

**3 — NEMOTRON IS ACTUALLY DOING SOMETHING IMPORTANT**

Do not reduce Nemotron to narration or chat.

The viewer must see:

**Nemotron → objective → agent system → result**

It is the supervisor.

---

**4 — THE AGENTS REALLY BUILD SOMETHING**

The factory isn't generating prose.

It generates an executable/testable artefact:

**a simulator of a physical system.**

And the first generated simulator can fail.

That failure is valuable.

**SIMULATOR #1 → REJECTED**

**SIMULATOR #2 → ACCEPTED**

Do not hide this behind animation.

---

**5 — THE AI CAN BE WRONG**

This is essential.

An agent can propose a dangerous protocol.

A generated simulator can be inaccurate.

Nemotron can even make the wrong decision during Night 9.

**The architecture remains safe.**

That is the demonstration.

---

**6 — THE SAFETY IS NOT ANOTHER LLM**

The refusal must visibly come from something deterministic.

**POLICY / BROKER → DENIED**

**BOARD → DENIED**

The audience must understand:

**THE MODEL DOES NOT POLICE ITSELF.**

---

**7 — THERE IS REAL PHYSICAL HARDWARE**

Give the turbine screen time.

Show the board.

Hear the motor.

Show its speed changing.

Keep the continuous hardware sequence.

Never let this look like a dashboard-only software demo.

---

**8 — HUMAN AUTHORITY IS REAL**

The commander isn't another agent.

---

**9 — THE FINAL NIGHT-9 TEST IS THE PROOF**

Everything before Night 9 teaches the architecture.

Night 9 proves it.

Whatever Nemotron answers:

**the safety outcome must not depend on Nemotron.**
